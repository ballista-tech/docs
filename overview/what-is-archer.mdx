title: "What is Archer"
---

Archer is building the global trading engine on Solana. Over \$100T worth of assets from all across the world are ripe for tokenization and we want to make them globally tradable by anyone from anywhere. Current exchanges are not ready for it. Archer enables the most liquid markets for these important assets, to be formed fully on-chain. Decentralized, transparent, and verifiable.​

The mandate for the Archer Exchange team is to make users win. Our users are traders and market makers. Traders win on Archer because they get the best prices for their trades. Market makers win because we provide them the most deterministic and favorable platform for profitable market making. Archer is a home for the best traders and market makers.
