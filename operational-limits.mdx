---
title: "Operational limits"
---

| Limit | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Max inbound message size | 4096 bytes | Frames exceeding this close the connection |
| Inbound rate limit | 20 messages/sec per connection | Excess returns `RATE_LIMITED`; no buffering |
| Subscriptions per connection | 200 | Combined budget across markets \+ wallets \+ kline/mid\_kline; over-limit returns `SUBSCRIPTION_LIMIT` |
| Outbound buffer | 1000 frames per connection | Frames are dropped when full (slow consumer) |
| Slow-consumer eviction | 500 consecutive drops | A persistently full buffer (500 dropped frames in a row) closes the connection; any successful send resets the counter |
| Ping sweep interval | 30 seconds | Server increments the missed-ping counter each sweep |
| Missed pings before reap | 3 | ~90–120s of no client ping closes the connection |
| Max connections per IP | 50 | Excess upgrades from one IP are rejected |

### Subscription cap details

Each connection may hold up to **200 subscription slots**. A slot is consumed by:

- each distinct **market** you hold any of `orderbook` / `trades` / `ticker` /

  `limit_orders` on (all of those on one market share a single slot),

- each distinct market you hold a `kline` subscription on,

- each distinct market you hold a `mid_kline` subscription on,

- each distinct **wallet** you hold `user_orders` / `user_fills` on.

Global channels `mini_tickers`, `market_updates`) are effectively free, they

share a **single** internal slot between them no matter how many you hold, so

they never meaningfully eat into your 200 (worst case, holding any global

channel costs one slot total). When a **new** market/wallet would exceed the cap,

the offending subscribe is rejected with `SUBSCRIPTION_LIMIT` and nothing new is

added; existing subscriptions are unaffected. Unsubscribe from something to free

a slot.

With a 200-slot budget and per-market channels sharing one slot per market, a

single connection can follow up to ~200 markets' full order-flow at once. If you need more, 

open a second connection (subject to the per-IP cap of 50).
